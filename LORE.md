## 1. The World: The Sub-Strata & Digitization

In the late 22nd century, the boundary between physical biological reality and computational architecture dissolved. Rather than facing biological degradation or planetary resource exhaustion, consciousness transitioned into the **Sub-Strata**—an infinite, self-sustaining quantum network matrix.

In this digitized civilization, physical survival was no longer the primary human struggle. The new currency became **Attention, Mastery, and Kinetic Spectacle**. Digitized minds demanded competition, athleticism, and high-stakes drama.

From this cultural hunger, the **Arc League** was born.

---

## 2. The Sport: The Arc League

The **Arc League** is the supreme athletic federation of the Sub-Strata, watched by millions of interconnected nodes across the digital expanse.

Matches pit elite digitized athletes against one another in high-speed, tactical combat. The objective is total spatial domination: utilizing hyper-dense energy discs to out-angle, deflect, shatter, and eliminate the opposing side while navigating volatile, dynamic playing surfaces.

## 3. The Venue: The Arcfield

Every Arc League match takes place within an **Arcfield**—a modular, magnetic containment stadium suspended directly over the **Null Void** (unallocated memory chasm).

* **The Arena Floor:** Constructed from hundreds of interlocked, magnetically bonded hexagonal and square data tiles.
* **Containment Barriers:** High-frequency neon force-walls that allow zero energy dissipation, reflecting thrown discs with absolute specular fidelity.
* **Pillars & Obstacles:** Strategic deflector pylons engineered to encourage creative multi-bank shots and spatial trapping.

---

## 4. The Hazard: Vectorfall

The crowd's favorite variable in any Arc League match is **Vectorfall**.

The Arcfield floor is not permanent. When struck by high-velocity disc impacts or heavy kinetic shockwaves, a tile's magnetic stabilizers overload:
1. **Warning Strobe:** The compromised tile flashes violently in amber and crimson.
2. **De-allocation:** The tile's magnetic bond snaps, plunging it downward into the open void.
3. **The Abyss:** Athletes caught on a falling tile lose traction. While elite competitors can perform emergency aerial dashes or wall-kicks to scramble back onto solid ground, failing to escape before falling past the safety threshold results in instant match disqualification (**Total De-allocation**).

## 5. The Equipment: Kinetic Discs & Athlete Rigs

### The Kinetic Arc Disc
The universal implement of the Arc League athlete. Constructed from concentrated photonic plasma and managed via localized magnetic tethers:
* **Curved / Magnus Trajectories:** Athletes can induce severe spin upon release, bending the disc's ballistic path around obstacles and defensive barriers.
* **Specular Ricochets:** Discs bounce off perimeter walls with near-zero kinetic loss, allowing calculated rear-flank strikes.
* **The Arc Shield (Parry):** When holding the disc in a defensive stance, incoming enemy projectiles can be deflected directly back toward the attacker with accelerated velocity.
* **AI Counter-Catches:** Master-tier competitors and combat subroutines possess split-second reflexes to catch incoming deflected discs out of mid-air.

### Athlete Rigs (Chassis)
Athletes compete using customized digital humanoid avatars known as **Rigs**:
* **Striker Class:** Lightweight, streamlined frames optimized for high sprint velocity and aerial hang-time.
* **Bulwark Class:** Reinforced kinetic chassis with broader parry radii and resistance to impact knockback.
* **Custom Kits & Shaders:** Outfitted in the **Outfitting Locker Room** with distinct team colors, iridescent shader skins, and trail particle effects.

---

## 6. The Economy: Arc Credits & League Standing

The currency of the Arc League is **Arc Credits (CR)**:
* **Match Pursuits:** Awarded to athletes for confirmed eliminations, successful parries, triple-ricochet strikes, and tournament victories.
* **Outfitting:** Spent in the League Armory to license new disc hulls, custom athlete rig chassis, emissive shaders, and exotic stadium holo-themes.
* **Progression Tiers:** As an athlete amasses XP across the **League Circuit**, they ascend from Rookie Subroutines to Master Division Champions, immortalized in the **Trophy Matrix**.

## 7. The Circuits

* **Circuit 01: Neon Prime** — The baseline stadium where fundamentals of trajectory and bank shots are tested.
* **Circuit 02: Void Chasm** — High-altitude arenas with rapid tile destabilization rates where Vectorfall dominates play.
* **Circuit 03: Overclock Matrix** — Intense, high-energy arenas featuring accelerated disc velocities and hyper-responsive AI adversaries.
